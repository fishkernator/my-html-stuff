The Porch

A Fiction-Writing Primer; being a Course in the Making of Stories, in Five Parts

This primer gathers, in five short parts, the whole of a craft that is commonly supposed to require none. It is meant to be worked through in order, and slowly, after the manner of a course of exercises rather than a course of lectures.

Each part takes up one labour of the story-teller and sets beside it a task to be performed by the reader's own hand; for it is held throughout these pages that the art is learned only in the doing, and that to read of writing without writing is to study swimming upon a chair.

What follows is therefore less a book to be admired than a bench to be worked at. The reader is invited to begin with the Foreword, and thence to the first discourse, and to keep whatever he makes.
