A free fiction-writing course

Learn to write fiction, from the ground up.

Most writing advice is a pile of disconnected tips. This is not that. Inkwork is a structured curriculum that treats fiction the way a drawing course treats drawing: as a set of concrete, learnable fundamentals, practiced in order, each one building on the last. You start with the sentence and you end with a finished story — and at every step you know exactly what you are practicing and why.

Work through the units in sequence. Each lesson explains one idea, shows it in action, and ends with an exercise you actually do. Do the exercises. Reading about writing is not writing.

How to use this course

Go in order. Don't skip the fundamentals because they feel obvious — feeling obvious and being easy to do are very different things. Keep everything you write; you'll revisit it later.
